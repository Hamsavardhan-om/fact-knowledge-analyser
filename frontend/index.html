Important facts are scattered across corporate filings, institutional reports, and statutory disclosures—often stated in different formats, separated by reporting vintages, or seemingly in conflict.

EFKL is an autonomous, schema-agnostic engine that decomposes unstructured multi-page PDFs into grounded Epistemic Fact Atoms, binds every assertion to verbatim source coordinates, and systematically evaluates cross-document relationships across Corroboration, Genuine Contradictions, Contextual Resolution (Time, Scope, Units), and Honest Error Audits.
